CipherLab

Interactive Symmetric Cryptography Laboratory

Explore five classical symmetric ciphers through interactive encryption and decryption. Enter a message, provide the required key, and observe how each algorithm transforms the data step by step.

Algorithms implemented: Playfair • Hill • Vigenère • One-Time Pad • Rail Fence
